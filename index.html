ABOUT

AN ARTIST
EXPLORING.

Etimu Jotham is an artist and 3D generalist based in Kampala, Uganda. His work moves between traditional art, digital 3D, motion and visual design.

The portfolio is intentionally open-ended: a place for finished work, school projects, experiments and the ideas that connect them.
